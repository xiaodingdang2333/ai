# Workflow Preferences

- For work in this directory, default to commercially viable Zhihu Yanyuan submission standards.
- Prioritize reader retention, a strong opening hook, clear emotional payoff, suitable pacing, and publishable quality.
- Keep answers concise and action-oriented unless the user asks for detailed explanation.
- When a Zhihu Yanyuan answer uses numbered section headings, leave two blank lines before and two blank lines after each numbered heading.
