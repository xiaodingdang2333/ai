# Scoring Rubric

Score evidence, not adjectives. Every score must have a one-sentence justification.

| Dimension | Max | Full-score evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Originality | 25 | Two or three load-bearing mechanisms are uncommon, coherent, and not surface swaps of familiar plots. |
| Emotional promise | 20 | The requested feeling is delivered in the opening and has a repeatable payoff mechanism. |
| Opening hook | 15 | The protagonist, abnormal rule, immediate choice, and consequence are clear within the first chapter premise. |
| Serialization fertility | 15 | At least five different units can escalate through distinct conflicts rather than repeated faces and settings. |
| Heroine agency | 10 | The heroine makes consequential choices and owns a useful action engine. |
| Romantic chemistry | 10 | Attraction grows through specific reciprocal choices; preference is visible and costly when the genre requires it. |
| Fanqie fit | 5 | Title/premise are quickly legible, the contrast is strong, and chapter-level payoffs can be frequent. |

## Hard Gates

- Total: at least 75/100.
- Originality: at least 18/25.
- Emotional promise: at least 16/20.
- No prohibited pattern breach.
- No genre bait-and-switch.
- Must support five materially different units.

## Calibration

- `90-100`: exceptional; ready for title collision checks and shortlist review.
- `82-89`: strong; likely shortlist material with a named mitigation.
- `75-81`: viable but needs one structural improvement before selection.
- `<75`: reject or rebuild.

Do not award originality for changing only era, profession, names, system vocabulary, or the heroine's apparent strength level.
